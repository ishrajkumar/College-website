BCA - Bachelor of Computer Applications

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) is an undergraduate degree program focused on computer applications and information technology. It helps students develop knowledge of programming, databases, web development, computer systems and software development. The course also develops logical thinking and problem-solving skills for careers in the IT field.
