Eligibility Requirements

  • Applicant must have passed 12th/Intermediate or equivalent examination.
  • Applicant must fulfill the eligibility requirements of the selected course.
  • Required subjects must be studied where applicable.
  • Applicant must provide valid educational documents.
  • Admission is subject to the rules of the concerned university/college.

Admission Process

  1. Choose your desired course.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Register for admission.
  4. Fill in the application form.
  5. Enter personal and educational details.
  6. Upload/submit required documents.
  7. Review the application.
  8. Wait for selection/merit information.
  9. Complete admission formalities after selection.
